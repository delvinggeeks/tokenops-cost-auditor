Terms of Service

Effective 2026-07-17 · TokenOps Cost Auditor, a WitAura product

1. The service.

TokenOps Cost Auditor analyzes LLM API usage logs you upload and produces a cost-audit report. One paid audit covers one uploaded log set up to the published size limit.

2. Your data.

{# FR-23 canonical string, verbatim. This clause previously PARAPHRASED it ("nothing IS retained… YOUR DATA IS never used"), the same failure as Terms quoting a price we do not charge: a binding document restating a published promise in its own words. #}

You must have the right to upload the logs you submit. We process them solely to produce your report: logs are analyzed then deleted; nothing retained beyond 7 days; your logs and prompts are never used to train any model. Details in the privacy policy and DPA-lite.

In plain words: we read counts, not conversations. Your prompts and completions are never stored, and the file itself is gone within a week.

3. Estimates, not guarantees.

Savings figures are conservative estimates computed from your logs and published provider rates. They are decision support, not a promise of realized savings; model suitability requires your own quality evaluation.

4. Payment.

{# R-TERMS-SUBS (founder-approved 2026-07-24). Subscription mechanics stated plainly; the ladder describes exactly what R-Q11/12 implements. Every figure renders from the one price config, never inline. #}

Subscriptions renew automatically each month until cancelled. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the current period — you keep the plan you paid for until then. The Free plan never requires a card.

If a payment fails, the subscription is marked past due; access becomes read-only after 7 days and reverts to Free after 21 days. Your reports and archived statements stay readable on Free, and cancellation does not change the data lifecycle described in clause 2.

One-off audits are prepaid ({{ one_shot }} per audit unless agreed otherwise). Payment links are provided by Razorpay (INR) or Stripe (USD). Refund: if we cannot produce a report from your upload, we refund in full.

In plain words: nothing is charged without a card you entered, a failed payment never deletes your data, and you can leave at the end of any month.

5. Acceptable use.

No attempts to breach, overload, or reverse the service; no uploading data you lack rights to.

Service provided "as is"; our aggregate liability is capped at the greater of the amounts you paid to WitAura in the twelve months preceding the claim.

We may update these terms; material changes are dated here. Contact: support contact listed on the landing page.
