Upload logs — get your audit

Your data: “analyzed then deleted; nothing retained beyond 7 days; never used for training.”
What you get
  • A dollar-ranked list of waste findings — missing prompt caching, retry storms (the same request paid for again and again), oversized models, prompt bloat, unbounded output caps, and chatty agent loops (agents re-sending the same context in bursts of small calls).

  • A client-ready PDF and a private web report, delivered from a deterministic rules engine — no AI guesses anywhere in the math.

What we never do
  • No SDK to install, no proxy in your request path, no gateway migration.
