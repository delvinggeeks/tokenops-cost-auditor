O-2 — Roles & RBAC

Who can see and do things in the product. Roles never touch the customer's LLM traffic (X-01/X-02) and the audit engine stays role-blind (R-ORG). Single-tenant is unchanged: a solo user is the owner of their workspace-of-one.
